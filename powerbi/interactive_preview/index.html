EXECUTIVE STRATEGY BRIEFING

Cyclistic Bike-Share: Annual Growth & Membership Strategy

Key findings, strategic recommendations, and financial opportunity model for executive leadership.

01

The Dual Persona Reality

Cyclistic serves two distinctly polarized customer segments: Annual Members treat the fleet as urban rapid transit (12.8 min trips, 8 AM & 5 PM rush-hour peaks, high weekday consistency). Casual Riders treat the fleet as recreational leisure (28.5 min trips, weekend afternoon surges).

55.2% of rides are generated by existing subscribers
02

The Commuter Casual Opportunity

Analysis identifies 11,480 casual rides that occur strictly on Monday through Friday during peak morning (7-9 AM) and evening (4-7 PM) commute hours. These riders have identical travel behaviors to Annual Members but pay on a high-friction per-ride basis.

$1.36M Total addressable recurring revenue in this pool
03

Geographic Station Clustering

Top casual volume is concentrated along Lakefront leisure stations (Streeter Dr / Navy Pier, Millennium Park, Oak St Beach) where casual riders account for over 75% of volume. Commuter demand is concentrated around West Loop rail terminals (Clinton & Washington, Clinton & Madison).

54% of total network volume flows through top 10 stations

Strategic Roadmap for Commercial Execution

INITIATIVE 1 • DIGITAL MARKETING

Automated Commute Conversion Push

Trigger automated mobile in-app prompts after a casual user completes 3 weekday rush-hour trips: "You commuted 3x this week! Save $45/month with an Annual Membership."

INITIATIVE 2 • PHYSICAL ACTIVATION

Field Ambassador QR Kiosks

Deploy branded signage and conversion ambassadors at Streeter Dr & Grand Ave (Navy Pier) and Millennium Park during peak Friday afternoon and weekend hours offering 1st-month trial discounts.

INITIATIVE 3 • FLEET LOGISTICS

Predictive Net-Flow Rebalancing

Use the Station Net Flow metrics to dispatch morning rebalancing vans directly from surplus lakefront docks to deficit rail terminals (Ogilvie & Union Station) before 7:30 AM.

INITIATIVE 4 • PRODUCT PACKAGING

Spring Season Launch Pass

Launch targeted email and digital campaigns in mid-April right before the May–July summer ridership explosion, locking in annual commitments before tourist season.
